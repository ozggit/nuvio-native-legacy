LOCAL PLAYBACK · VERSION 0.1

Nuvio PC Audio

Keep browsing Kaptain and Bingecat in Nuvio. This add-on gets your AIOStreams results, copies the video and converts English multichannel audio to Dolby Digital 5.1 on this PC.

TV compatibility still needs a real playback test. Seeking is limited to a rolling buffer of about 12 minutes; jumping ahead, normal resume and long pauses are not supported yet. This version does not provide the full film timeline or embedded subtitle tracks. Stereo sources stay stereo.

1. Connect your AIOStreams

Paste the installation link from your saved AIOStreams configuration, ending in /manifest.json. It is saved on this PC and is never shown in logs. Keep AIOStreams’ own proxy setting off for this route.

2. Add PC Audio to Nuvio

Copy this address into Nuvio’s add-on installation field. Keep Kaptain and Bingecat. Initially keep your original AIOStreams add-on too, then choose a result labelled PC 5.1 when testing.

The PC must remain awake and connected to the same network as the TV. If Nuvio cannot connect, open the link on your phone while connected to the same Wi-Fi. The included firewall helper allows only this port from the local network.

Playback status

Dolby Vision and unusual remux profiles can still be rejected by the TV even with compatible audio. Video is never re-encoded. Stop a session to clear its temporary files.
